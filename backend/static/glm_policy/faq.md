# CryptoGLAME FAQ

## What is GLM?

GLM is a GLAME club utility token for CryptoGLAME scenarios: partner rewards, referral rewards, bridge operations, GLM Store purchases, selected access passes, the GLAME mobile app ecosystem and future GLAME online services.

GLM is not an investment product, bank deposit, electronic money, security, loan, or promise of income.

## How are GLM and 1C loyalty points different?

1C loyalty points are stored in the GLAME loyalty system and are used for ordinary loyalty purchases under active loyalty rules.

GLM is stored in a supported TON wallet as a Jetton. The platform reads the wallet balance, shows operations and keeps an audit trail, but the actual GLM balance is on-chain.

## What supports GLM utility?

GLM is connected to real GLAME retail activity through the loyalty program. Customers earn ordinary 1C loyalty points from real jewelry purchases in GLAME stores, and enabled bridge operations can convert points to GLM or GLM back to points under active rules.

This is utility support, not a promise of market price, ruble redemption, buyback, profit, or exchange liquidity.

## Can I earn GLM from referral purchases?

Yes, where GLAME enables it under the active referral program rules. A partner can invite a customer, the customer makes a purchase, and GLM can be credited after the purchase is confirmed and the required hold, anti-fraud and return checks pass.

## Can physical stores accept GLM directly?

No. The pilot model keeps physical stores on the ordinary 1C loyalty program. A user can convert GLM to 1C points first, then use points in a store according to the loyalty rules.

## How does "points -> GLM" work?

The user connects and verifies a TON wallet, creates a "points -> GLM" request, and the platform checks available 1C points.

If enough points are available and the hot-wallet has enough GLM and TON gas, the platform debits the points and sends GLM to the verified TON wallet automatically.

If there is a technical issue, the request stays in the operator queue for review, retry, or cancellation.

## How does "GLM -> points" work?

The user creates a request and sends GLM from the verified TON wallet to the GLAME treasury wallet.

After the TON transfer is found and checked, GLAME credits 1C loyalty points. These points follow the active 1C loyalty rules after issuance.

## What is GLM Store?

GLM Store is an online utility shop for branded goods, services, access passes and pilot offers.

Some items can be paid with 1C points. Some items can be paid with GLM through a TON transaction to the GLAME treasury wallet. Fulfillment happens after the payment is verified.

## Can GLM be transferred, bought, or sold outside GLAME?

GLM is designed as a TON Jetton, so blockchain transfers are technically possible where supported by the wallet/network.

GLAME does not guarantee public market liquidity, exchange listing, buyback, ruble redemption, USDT exchange, or future price growth.

## What rate is used inside GLAME?

The MVP reference rate for enabled utility operations is shown in the interface and current rules. For example, the pilot bridge can use 1 point -> 1 GLM or 1 GLM -> 1 point, while purchases of points can include a spread.

This internal reference is not a public market price guarantee.

## What fees or spread can apply?

GLAME can apply service spread, limits, minimum/maximum operation amounts, holds, anti-fraud checks and manual review.

The active terms must be shown in the product, bridge block, or public rules before the operation.

## What should I do if an operation is stuck?

Contact GLAME support and provide only safe public data:

- partner name or code;
- operation date and amount;
- TON wallet address;
- TON transaction hash, if it exists;
- screenshot of the operation status.

Never send a seed phrase, private key, wallet password, Telegram code, or one-time password to anyone.
