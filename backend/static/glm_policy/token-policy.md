# GLM Token Policy

GLM is a GLAME club utility unit used inside CryptoGLAME products, partner rewards, referral rewards, GLM Store, bridge operations, the GLAME mobile app ecosystem and selected loyalty scenarios.

GLM is not an investment product, deposit, security, electronic money, bank account, loan, or guaranteed claim against GLAME.

GLM can be used only where GLAME explicitly enables utility usage. GLAME may set limits, holds, lock periods, verification rules and anti-fraud controls.

Physical GLAME stores do not accept GLM directly. In-store purchases use ordinary 1C loyalty points; GLM can be converted to points through an approved bridge before the purchase.

GLM is connected to real GLAME retail operations through the loyalty program: ordinary 1C points are earned from real jewelry purchases in GLAME stores and can be bridged to GLM under active rules. GLM can also be bridged back to 1C points before an in-store purchase.

Partners may receive GLM for confirmed referral purchases under the active referral program rules. Referral GLM may be subject to hold, anti-fraud checks, returns policy, limits and manual review.

This loyalty connection is utility support, not financial collateral, a redemption guarantee, a public price guarantee, or an obligation for GLAME to buy GLM back.

GLM is intended to be held in a supported blockchain wallet, currently TON Jetton in the pilot architecture. GLAME Platform may keep an operational ledger for requests, holds, audit, transaction hashes and reconciliation.

The phrase "1 GLM = 1 ruble inside GLAME" means an internal product/service reference under active program rules and limits. It is not a public price guarantee, buyback promise or fixed exchange rate.

1C loyalty points and GLM are separate entities. Conversion between them happens only through approved bridge operations.
