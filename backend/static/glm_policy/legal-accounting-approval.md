# CryptoGLAME Legal and Accounting Approval

Status: approval template for production sign-off  
Updated: 2026-07-07

This document defines what legal and accounting must approve before CryptoGLAME can move from controlled mainnet technical pilot to public partner usage.

## Scope

The approval covers:

- GLM Token Policy;
- GLM Risk Disclosure;
- GLM Bridge Rules;
- GLM landing page wording;
- partner offer and referral program wording;
- accounting treatment for GLM bridge, store, refunds and loyalty points purchase.

This approval does not approve DEX listing, public liquidity provision, buyback, investment promotion, fixed exchange rate, guaranteed price support, USDT cash-out or securities-like distribution.

## Legal Position

GLM is positioned as a utility token for GLAME partner and loyalty scenarios.

GLM is not positioned as:

- an investment product;
- a security;
- electronic money;
- a bank deposit;
- a loan;
- a guaranteed ruble claim;
- a buyback obligation;
- a promise of growth, yield or liquidity.

Public materials must not promise that GLM price will grow, that GLAME will redeem GLM for rubles or USDT, or that external markets will provide liquidity.

## Loyalty Connection

GLM is connected to real GLAME operations through controlled utility mechanics:

- partners can earn GLM from confirmed referral purchases;
- users can convert eligible 1C loyalty points to GLM under bridge rules;
- users can convert GLM to 1C loyalty points under bridge rules;
- GLM can be used in GLM Store and other approved GLAME utility scenarios.

This is a product and loyalty utility model. It is not collateral, a reserve guarantee or a public price guarantee.

## Accounting Model To Approve

The finance/accounting owner must approve handling for these operation types:

| Flow | Business event | Required accounting decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `points_to_glm` | Loyalty points are spent, GLM is sent from hot-wallet | How to record points spend, GLM distribution and any internal liability reduction. |
| `glm_to_points` | GLM is received by treasury, 1C points are issued | How to record incoming GLM utility receipt and issued loyalty points. |
| `buy_loyalty_points` | User pays GLM plus spread for 1C points | How to record spread, issued points and treasury GLM. |
| GLM Store | User pays GLM for branded goods/service/access | How to record revenue, inventory/service fulfillment and treasury GLM. |
| Refund | GLM or points are returned/corrected | How to record reversal, treasury transfer and 1C correction. |
| Referral GLM | Partner receives GLM for confirmed referral purchase | How to record referral expense/reward and hold release. |

## KYC/AML and Anti-Fraud

Before public launch, GLAME must approve:

- thresholds for manual review of large or repeated operations;
- suspicious activity triggers;
- blocked wallet/address policy;
- partner identity requirements;
- sanctions/AML escalation owner;
- refund and chargeback handling;
- data retention period for wallet, tx hash and operation audit.
