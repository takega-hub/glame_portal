# CryptoGLAME Treasury Policy Draft

Status: internal draft for finance/legal review  
Last updated: 2026-07-02

## 1. Scope

This policy covers GLM treasury, escrow, burn, reserve and future liquidity operations.

## 2. Principles

- No treasury operation without an internal reason code.
- No public liquidity launch before legal and security approval.
- No market-making promise or price support promise.
- No customer-facing buyback obligation unless separately approved in writing.
- Treasury balances and operations must be auditable.

## 3. Treasury Buckets

- Utility reserve: GLM reserved for GLM Store, services and campaigns.
- Bridge escrow: GLM received or reserved for `glm_to_points` and `buy_loyalty_points`.
- Burn bucket: GLM removed from circulation after utility redemption or policy-defined burn.
- Operational treasury: GLM held for controlled pilots and corrections.
- Future liquidity bucket: locked until legal/security approval.

## 4. Approval Matrix

- Routine internal journal/audit hash publication: product admin.
- Bridge repair and manual 1C document marking: admin with audit comment.
- Treasury transfer, liquidity provision or external wallet operation: finance + legal + owner approval.
- Mainnet contract deployment: owner + legal + security approval.

## 5. Reporting

Weekly internal report should include:

- issued GLM;
- burned GLM;
- GLM in hold;
- GLM in bridge escrow;
- GLM Store redemptions;
- failed/canceled bridge refunds;
- public audit hash status.
