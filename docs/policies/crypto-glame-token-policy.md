# CryptoGLAME GLM Token Policy Draft

Status: internal draft for legal review  
Last updated: 2026-07-02

## 1. Purpose

GLM is a GLAME club utility unit used inside CryptoGLAME products, partner rewards, GLM Store, bridge operations and selected loyalty scenarios.

GLM is designed as a utility and accounting layer for GLAME services. It is not positioned as an investment product, deposit, security, electronic money, bank account, loan, or guaranteed claim against GLAME.

## 2. Core Rules

- GLM can be earned through approved GLAME actions, referral operations and bridge operations.
- GLM can be used only where GLAME explicitly enables utility usage.
- GLM is intended to be held in a supported blockchain wallet, currently TON Jetton in the pilot architecture. GLAME Platform may keep an operational ledger for requests, holds, audit, transaction hashes and reconciliation.
- GLM does not create an automatic right to receive cash, rubles, USDT, TON or other assets from GLAME.
- GLAME may set limits, holds, lock periods, verification rules and anti-fraud controls.
- GLAME may reverse, freeze or adjust GLM related to fraud, chargebacks, returns, technical mistakes or policy violations.
- Physical GLAME stores do not accept GLM directly. In-store purchases use ordinary 1C loyalty points; GLM can be converted to points through an approved bridge before the purchase.

## 3. Internal Value

The phrase "1 GLM = 1 ruble inside GLAME" means an internal product/service reference under active program rules and limits. It is not a public price guarantee, buyback promise or fixed exchange rate.

GLAME may accept GLM for:

- GLM Store items;
- service privileges;
- bridge operations to loyalty points;
- selected campaigns;
- future partner network scenarios.

## 4. Bonus Points Separation

1C loyalty points and GLM are separate entities.

- 1C points live by the loyalty program rules and may expire.
- GLM lives in the CryptoGLAME ledger and does not expire by calendar unless a special promo rule says otherwise.
- Conversion between the two happens only through approved bridge operations.

## 5. On-chain Phase

Before public on-chain GLM launch, GLAME must complete:

- legal review;
- security review;
- treasury policy approval;
- risk disclosure publication;
- KYC/AML rules for applicable scenarios.
