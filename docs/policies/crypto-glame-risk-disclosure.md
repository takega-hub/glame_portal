# CryptoGLAME Risk Disclosure Draft

Status: internal/public draft for legal review  
Last updated: 2026-07-02

## Important Notice

CryptoGLAME and GLM are experimental utility features. Participation may be limited, changed, paused or discontinued.

This document is a product disclosure draft and must be reviewed by legal counsel before public launch.

## Main Risks

- GLM is not an investment and GLAME does not promise growth of GLM value.
- GLAME does not guarantee buyback, cash-out, ruble redemption, USDT exchange or listing on any exchange.
- Market price, if P2P or DEX trading is later enabled, may be lower or higher than the internal GLAME reference.
- External transfers may be irreversible.
- Wallet access, private keys and seed phrases are the user's responsibility.
- Blockchain networks may have delays, fees, outages or technical failures.
- GLAME may block, delay or reverse operations linked to suspected fraud, technical error, return, chargeback or policy violation.
- Bridge operations may require manual review and may not be instant.
- Loyalty points received through bridge are governed by loyalty rules active at the moment of issuance.

## Communication Rules

Do not use statements such as:

- "GLM will grow";
- "GLM is guaranteed";
- "GLM is backed by rubles";
- "GLAME will buy GLM back";
- "GLM is risk-free";
- "GLM is a stablecoin";
- "GLM is equal to money".

Allowed safer language:

- "GLM is a club utility unit";
- "GLM can be used inside GLAME under program rules";
- "GLM has an internal reference for selected GLAME services";
- "GLM bridge operations are subject to limits and review".
